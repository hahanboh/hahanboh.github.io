## 第一讲 简单句
### 核心构成
句子主干 五种基本句型
1. <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Water</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">is</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">a raw material</span> in the vital process of photosynthesis. 【主系表】
2. <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Methane</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">forms</span> in large concentration above wetlands. (TL20) 【主谓】
3. Nearly all astronomical <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">objects</span> in space <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">emit</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">radio waves</span>. (TL19) 【主谓宾】
4. <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">This job</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">gave</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Smith</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">an opportunity</span> to study the fresh rock. 【主谓宾宾】
5. A closely related <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">theory</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">sees</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">theater</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">as evolving out of dances</span>. 【主谓宾宾补】
谓语动词单一性原则：在一个句子中，有且只有一个谓语动词
I am a student study economics. (x)
I am a student <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">studying economics</span> (√)
### 修饰成分
非谓语动词 
	现在分词 doing 主动
	过去分词 done 被动
	不定式 to do 目的
#### 1.定语：修饰名词
（1）a <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">foolish</span> man (2)jolin <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">in the house</span>后置定语
结构： 前置定语+名词+后置定语【<mark class="colorez-highlight" style="--hl-color: var(--color-red);">左二右六</mark>】
##### 前置定语（2种形式）
(1) <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">The</span> book is interesting.(限定词:a,an,the,this,my)
(2) The <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">cute</span> girl is my sister.(形容词)
##### 后置定语（6种形式）
(1) The young boy <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">handsome enough to play the lead role</span> is Justin Bieber.(<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">形容词</span>短语)
(2) The actor <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">with a big nose</span> is Jackie Chan.(<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">介词</span>短语)
(3) The lucky man <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">to do what he likes</span> is Trump.(<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">不定式</span>短语)
(4) The star <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">wearing a beard</span> is Brad Pitt.(<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">现在分词</span>短语)
(5) The celebrity <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">regarded as a tough woman</span> is Jennifer Aniston.(<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">过去分词</span>短语)
(6) Power is the old stone building <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that stands for centuries</span>.(<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">定语</span>从句)

##### 定语与状语对照
同样的介词短语，要看它修饰什么。
- The boy <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">in the room</span> is reading. → 修饰boy，作定语：房间里的男孩正在读书。
- The boy is reading <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">in the room</span>. → 说明阅读的地点，作状语：男孩正在房间里读书。
#### 2，状语：修饰词或句子
(1) He runs <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">quickly</span>.（副词）
(2) We study English <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">on the internet</span>.（介词短语）
(3) <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">To serve the people well</span>, he studies hard.（不定式短语）
(4) <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Entering the city</span>, they saw a lot of soldiers marching.（现在分词短语）
(5) <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Seen from the top of the hill</span>, the city is more beautiful.（过去分词短语）
##### 分词状语的逻辑主语
普通分词短语作状语时，其逻辑主语通常与主句主语一致；判断时先问“谁做这个动作”或“谁承受这个动作”。
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Entering the city</span>, **they** saw soldiers. → they进入城市，与enter构成主动关系。
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Seen from the hill</span>, **the city** looks beautiful. → city被看见，与see构成被动关系。
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Having finished his work</span>, **he** left. → he先完成工作，再离开；having done表示该动作先于主句动作完成。

例: Reykjavik, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the capital of Iceland</span>, is entirely heated by geothermal energy <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">derived from volcanic heat</span>.

## 第二讲 并列句
### 一、并列句构成
  1. 结构:简单句1+并列连词+简单句2
  例: <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">Ice sheets</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">retreated</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">and</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">sea levels</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">rose</span>.(TR20)
  2. 并列连词：【<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">FAN BOYS</span>】
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">for</span>：因为（补充解释原因）
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">and</span>：和；并且（并列、递进）。both...and...：……和……都；not only...but also：不仅……而且……
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">nor</span>：也不（否定并列）。neither...nor...：既不……也不……
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">but</span>：但是（转折）。yet：然而；while、whereas：而、然而（表示对比）
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">or</span>：或者（选择）；否则。either...or...：要么……要么……
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">yet</span>：然而；但是（转折）
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">so</span>：所以；因此（结果）
练习
1. <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">There</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">were</span> no bears on the islands, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">yet</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">the island’s cedars</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">are</span> still in decline. (TS23)
2. Probably <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">the most important environmental factor</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">is</span> temperature, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">for</span> if <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">the growing season</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">is</span> too short <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">and</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">temperatures</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">are</span> too low, <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">tree shoots</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">and</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">buds</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">cannot mature</span> sufficiently to survive the winter months. (TR1)
### 二、并列句的省略
1. 省略条件：并列句中，<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">相同的并列成分</span>，可省略。
例：<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">The engine</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">helped</span> solve the problem of draining coal mines of groundwater <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">and increased</span> the production of coal. (TR26)

## 第三讲 复合句
### 复合句的分类

| 从句类别 | 相当于的词性 | 细分 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <span style="color: var(--color-red)">名词性从句</span> | 名词（N.） | 主语从句、<span style="color: var(--color-red)">宾语从句</span>、表语从句、同位语从句 |
| 定语从句 | 形容词（Adj.） | — |
| 状语从句 | 副词（Adv.） | — |
### 复合句的构成
一、内容：
1. <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">主句</span>：1个（<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">主要</span>）
2. 从句：多个（次要）
二、形式：
1. 主句 + <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">引导词</span> + 从句
2. <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">引导词</span> + 从句，主句
### 一、名词性从句
1. 定义：把<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">一个句子（从句）</span>当做<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">名词</span>来用，放在<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">另外一个句子（主句）</span>当中，充当主语、宾语、表语、同位语。
2. 结构：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">引导词</span> + 从句（<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">陈述语序</span>）
3. 引导词：that，whether，<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">if</span>，when，where，why，how，what ...
4. 演示：

| 类别    | 例句                                                                                                   | 说明 |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| 主语    | <span style="color: var(--color-red)">This answer</span> is right.                                   | 名词短语作主语 |
| 主语从句  | <span style="color: var(--color-red)">What you said</span> is right.                                 | 从句整体作主语 |
| 宾语    | I don’t know <span style="color: var(--color-red)">the answer</span>.                                | 名词短语作宾语 |
| 宾语从句  | I don’t know <span style="color: var(--color-red)">if you will come back</span>.                     | 从句整体作宾语 |
| 表语    | This issue is <span style="color: var(--color-red)">serious</span>.                                  | 形容词说明主语性质 |
| 表语从句  | This issue is <span style="color: var(--color-red)">that the environment should be protected</span>. | 从句说明主语内容 |
| 同位语   | I love the novel, <span style="color: var(--color-red)">Harry Potter</span>.                         | 名称说明是哪部小说 |
| 同位语从句 | I love the saying <span style="color: var(--color-red)">that love, not time heals all wounds</span>. | 从句解释名词内容 |

#### （一）宾语从句
1. 定义：<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">一个句子（从句）</span>放在另外一个句子（<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">主句</span>）中，作<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">宾语的位置</span>。
2. 结构：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">引导词</span> + <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">从句</span>（<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">陈述语序</span>）
3. 写法：由<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">三种类型的简单句</span>演变而来。

| 简单句类型 | 搭配的引导词                         | 简单句                 | 宾语从句                                                                                  |
| ----- | ------------------------------ | ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 陈述句   | that（无含义，可省略）                  | He is feeling ill.  | Mike says <span style="color: var(--color-red)">that he is feeling ill</span>.        |
| 一般疑问句 | if / whether（是否）               | Does she live here? | He wants to know <span style="color: var(--color-red)">whether she lives here</span>. |
| 特殊疑问句 | 特殊疑问词（who、what、when、where ...） | What does he like?  | I wonder <span style="color: var(--color-red)">what he likes</span>.                  |

#### （三）主语从句
1. 定义：<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">一个句子（从句）</span>放在另外一个句子（<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">主句</span>）中，作<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">主语的位置</span>。
2. 结构：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">引导词</span> + <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">从句</span>（<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">陈述语序</span>）
3. 写法：由<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">三种类型的简单句</span>演变而来。

| 简单句类型 | 搭配的引导词 | 简单句 | 主语从句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

#### （四）同位语从句
1. 定义：一个句子放在另外一个句子中，作<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">同位语的位置</span>，用来解释说明一个<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">抽象名词</span>。
2. 结构：<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">抽象名词</span> + <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">引导词</span> + 从句（<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">陈述语序</span>）
3. 写法：由<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">三种类型的简单句</span>演变而来。

| 简单句类型 | 搭配的引导词 | 简单句 | 同位语从句 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 陈述句 | that（无含义，不可以省略） | The earth is round. | The fact <span style="color: var(--color-red)">that the earth is round</span> is true. |
| 一般疑问句 | whether（是否） | Will he come? | We have argued over the question <span style="color: var(--color-red)">whether he will come</span>. |
| 特殊疑问句 | 特殊疑问词（who、what、when、where ...） | Why didn’t he come here? | We want to know the fact <span style="color: var(--color-red)">why he didn’t come here</span>. |

4. 位置：位于<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">抽象名词</span>之后，常见的抽象名词有：idea、opinion、fact、question、doubt、belief、possibility。

### 二、定语从句
1. 定义：一个句子作定语，<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">修饰限定名词</span>，置于所修饰的名词<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">之后</span>。
2. 结构：先行词 + 关系词 + 定语从句【不完整的陈述句】
3. 先行词：被<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">定语从句</span>所修饰的<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">名词</span>。
例1：I like <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">cats</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that are lovely</span>. → 修饰 cats：可爱的猫；that 在从句中作主语。
拆分：I like <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">cats</span>. + <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">Cats</span> are lovely.
#### 4. 关系词
**1）分类**
- 关系代词：that、which、who、whom、whose。
- 关系副词：when、where、why。
**2）作用**
连接、替代、作成分。
例1：I like <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">cats</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that are lovely</span>. → 修饰 cats：可爱的猫；that 在从句中作主语。
例2：I like <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">this cat</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that I met yesterday</span>. → 修饰 this cat：我昨天遇见的这只猫；that 作宾语。
**3）先行词与关系词**

| 先行词 | 关系词 |
| --- | --- |
| 物 | that / which |
| 人 | that / who / whom |
| 人 / 物（所有关系） | whose |
| 时间 | when |
| 地点 | where |
| 原因 | why |
##### ① 先行词是物：that / which
例：I like <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">cats</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that are lovely</span>. → 修饰 cats：可爱的猫；that 在从句中作主语。
托福实战演练

#### （一）限定性定语从句与非限定性定语从句
定语从句分为：限定性定语从句和非限定性定语从句。

| 区别 | 限定性定语从句 | 非限定性定语从句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 逗号 | 无 | 有 |
| 作用 | 修饰限定，<span style="color: var(--color-red)">不可省略</span> | 补充说明，<span style="color: var(--color-red)">可以省略</span> |
| 翻译 | 需<span style="color: var(--color-red)">提至先行词之前</span> | 按<span style="color: var(--color-red)">顺序翻译</span> |
| 关系词 | 可以用 that | <span style="color: var(--color-red)">不可以用 that</span> |

例：This is my girl friend who is working in Paris. → 限定是哪一位：在巴黎工作的那位女友。
This is my girl friend<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">,</span> who is working in Paris. → 补充说明这位女友在巴黎工作。
#### （二）特殊定语从句
##### 1. 介词提前的定语从句
托福实战演练

### 三、状语从句
1. 定义：一个句子作状语，相当于<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">副词</span>。
2. 结构：
- 主句 + 引导词 + <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">状语从句</span>（完整的陈述句）
- 引导词 + <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">状语从句</span>，主句
3. 关键：<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">引导词</span>。
4. 分类：时间、地点、条件、原因、目的、结果、比较、方式和让步。

## 第四讲 特殊句型
### 一、强调句
#### 1. 结构
<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">It is / was</span> + <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">被强调部分</span> + <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that / who</span> + 句子其余部分。
正常句：J.K. Rowling wrote a magical novel in 1997.
① <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">It was</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">J.K. Rowling</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">who</span> wrote a magical novel in 1997. → 强调主语：写小说的人是 J.K. Rowling。
② <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">It was</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">a magical novel</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that</span> J.K. Rowling wrote in 1997. → 强调宾语：她写的是一本魔幻小说。
③ <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">It was</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">in 1997</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that</span> J.K. Rowling wrote a magical novel. → 强调时间状语：写小说是在1997年。
总结：
- <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">被强调部分</span>：主语、宾语、表语、状语。
- <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">不能被强调</span>的部分：形容词、动词。
思考：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">It is ... that ...</span> 结构一定是强调句吗？
#### 2. 托福实战演练
例1：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">It is</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">during the early stages of its development</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that</span> a society becomes aware of forces. (TR1) → 强调句，强调时间：在社会发展的早期阶段。
例2：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">It is</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">probable</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that</span> as early as 200 B.C. Teotihuacan may have achieved some religious significance. (TR8) → 主语从句后置；it 是形式主语，that 从句说明“很可能”的内容。
判断方法：去掉 <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">It is / was ... that / who</span>，其余部分能还原成完整句子，通常就是强调句。
### 二、倒装句
#### 1. 定义
“主谓倒装”：<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">谓语动词放在主语之前</span>。
#### 2. 分类
- 全部倒装：谓语动词<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">全部</span>置于主语之前。
- 部分倒装：谓语动词<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">一部分</span>置于主语之前。
#### （一）全部倒装
##### ① 表—系—主
托福实战演练
例1：<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">Among the key factors behind this growth</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">were</span> increased agricultural productivity and an expansion of trade. (TR10) → 系动词 were 在主语前，主语是农业生产力提高和贸易扩大。
正常语序：Increased agricultural productivity and an expansion of trade <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">were</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">among the key factors behind this growth</span>.
##### ② 时间、方式、伴随状语位于句首
托福实战演练
例2：<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">With the increase in population and the development of centralized authority</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">came</span> the emergence of the cities. (TR33) → came 在主语前，主语是 the emergence of the cities（城市的出现）。
正常语序：The emergence of the cities <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">came</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">with the increase in population and the development of centralized authority</span>.
#### （二）部分倒装
##### ① 否定副词或词组位于句首
<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">never、little、hardly、seldom、not until、under no circumstances</span>。
托福实战演练
例1：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Not until</span> the eighteenth century <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">did</span> such banks begin to provide capital for business investment. (TR10) → Not until 提前，助动词 did 放在主语 such banks 前；直到18世纪才开始提供资金。
正常语序：Such banks <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">did not</span> begin to provide capital for business investment <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">until</span> the eighteenth century. (TR10)
##### ② Only + 状语位于句首
简短对照：
- <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">Only then</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">did</span> he understand. → only 修饰时间状语，要倒装。
- <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">Only he</span> understood. → only 修饰主语，不倒装。
托福实战演练
例2：<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">Only after</span> several inadequate breaths under such conditions <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">is</span> breathing effort <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">adjusted</span>. (TR24) → Only after 引出时间状语，is 放在主语 breathing effort 前。
正常语序：Breathing effort <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">is adjusted</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">only after</span> several inadequate breaths under such conditions.
### 三、虚拟语气
#### 1. 定义
虚拟语气：表示<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">与事实相反的假设</span>、<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">可能性较低的设想</span>，或建议、要求等。
#### 2. 形式
<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">谓语动词</span>通过“<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">改变时态</span>”表达虚拟语气。
#### （一）if 条件句的虚拟
1. 结构：“<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">后退一步</span>”。

| 时间 | if 从句 | 主句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 与现在相反 | did / were | 主语 + would / should / could / might + <span style="color: var(--color-red)">do</span> |
| 与过去相反 | had done | 主语 + would / should / could / might + <span style="color: var(--color-red)">have done</span> |
| 与将来相反 | did / were；were to do；should do | 主语 + would / should / could / might + <span style="color: var(--color-red)">do</span> |

2. 托福实战演练
例1：If I <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">were</span> you, I <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">would be</span> upset too. (TL8) → 与现在相反：假如我是你，我也会难过。
例2：If element 43 ever <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">had been</span> present on Earth, it <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">would have decayed</span> ages ago. (TL8) → 对过去的假设：如果43号元素曾存在于地球上，它早已衰变。
例3：If the United States government <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">were to stop</span> helping producers, the price of ethanol <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">would increase</span> greatly. (TL8) → 对将来的假设：如果政府停止援助生产商，乙醇价格会大涨。
#### （二）名词性从句的虚拟
1. 规则：名词性从句（主语、宾语、表语、同位语）对应的<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">主句</span>中，有表示“<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">建议</span>”“<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">命令</span>”“<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">要求</span>”等词时，<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">名词性从句需要虚拟</span>。
2. 结构：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">should + 动词原形</span>（<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">should 可以省略</span>）。
3. 托福实战演练
例：Mendeleyev suggested that the atomic mass of indium <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">be changed</span> to a completely different value. (TR16) → suggested 表示“建议”，be changed 前省略了 should，表示建议修改铟的原子质量。

## 第五讲 长难句分析
### 一、入门示例
He said <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> that “that” <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> that man used is wrong.
- 主干：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">He said ...</span>；宾语从句主干：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">that “that” is wrong</span>。
- 五个 that 依次是：宾语从句引导词、指示词“那个”、被当作词语讨论的“that”、定语从句关系词、指示词“那个”。
- that that man used 修饰被讨论的“that”。→ 他说，那个人使用的那个“that”是错的。
### 二、“3拆2看”原则
#### 1. 三拆
① <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">介词</span>：of、with、in、for、to 等，划出介词短语。
② <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">引导词</span>：that、whether、which、what 等，划出从句。
③ <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">非谓语动词</span>：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">to do</span>、doing、<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">done（被动）</span>，划出非谓语短语。
#### 2. 两看
① <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">主句</span>：重点，先找主干。
② <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">从句</span>：辅助，再看从句与主句的关系。
- 名词性从句：主语、宾语、表语、同位语从句。
- 定语从句。
- 状语从句。
### 三、示范分析
Last year, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the influential trade show</span> Designers & Agents <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">stopped</span> charging its participation fee for young green entrepreneurs <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">who</span> attend its two springtime shows in Los Angeles and New York, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">and gave special recognition</span> to designers <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">whose</span> collections are at least 25% sustainable.
- 主干：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">The trade show stopped charging its participation fee and gave special recognition to designers.</span> → 展会停止收取参展费，并给予设计师特别认可。
- who attend ... 修饰 young green entrepreneurs；whose collections ... 修饰 designers。
- charging ... 作 stopped 的宾语；Last year 表示时间，in Los Angeles and New York 表示展会举办地点。
### 四、托福实战演练
#### 例1
Totally <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">without</span> light and <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">subjected</span> to intense pressures hundreds of times greater <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">than</span> at the Earth’s surface,  <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the deep-ocean bottom is a hostile environment</span> to humans, in some ways <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">as</span> forbidding and remote <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">as</span> the void of outer space.
- 主干：The deep-ocean bottom is a hostile environment to humans. → 深海海底对人类来说环境恶劣。
- 拆分：句首 without light 和 subjected to ... 说明海底无光、承受高压；hundreds of times greater than ... 修饰 pressures。
- 比较：as forbidding and remote as ... 将深海海底与外太空相比。
#### 例2
<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Basic</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">to</span> any understanding of Canada <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">in</span> the 20 years <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">after</span> the Second World War <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">is</span> <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the country's impressive population growth</span>.
- 正常语序：The country's impressive population growth is basic to any understanding of Canada in the 20 years after the Second World War.
- 拆分：表语 Basic ... 提前，形成“表—系—主”倒装；真正主语是 population growth。→ 人口增长是理解战后20年加拿大情况的基础。
#### 例3
As a result, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">claims</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> eating a diet <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">consisting</span> entirely of organically grown foods prevents or cures disease or provides other benefits to health <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">have become widely publicized and form the basis for folklore</span>.
- 主干：Claims have become widely publicized and form the basis for folklore. → 这些说法被广泛传播，并成为民间观念的基础。
- 拆分：that ... health 是同位语从句，说明 claims 的内容；从句主语是 eating a diet ...，谓语是 prevents or cures ... or provides ...。
- 修饰：consisting entirely of organically grown foods 修饰 diet，表示完全由有机食品组成的饮食。
#### 例4
<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">There are numerous unsubstantiated reports</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> natural vitamins are superior to synthetic ones, <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> fertilized eggs are nutritionally superior to unfertilized eggs, <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> untreated grains are better than fumigated grains and the like.
- 主干：There are numerous unsubstantiated reports. → 有大量未经证实的说法。
- 拆分：三个 that 引导并列的同位语从句，分别说明 reports 的内容：天然维生素、受精卵和未经处理的谷物更好。
#### 例5
<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">Large wind farms might also interfere with the flight patterns</span> of migratory birds in certain areas, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">and they have killed large birds</span> of prey (especially hawks, falcons, and eagles) <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> prefer to hunt along the same ridge lines <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> are ideal for wind turbines.
- 主干：Large wind farms might interfere with the flight patterns, and they have killed large birds of prey. → 风电场可能干扰候鸟飞行，也曾造成猛禽死亡。
- 拆分：第一个 that 从句修饰 birds of prey；第二个 that 从句修饰 ridge lines，定语从句中又包含定语从句。
- 括号：especially hawks, falcons, and eagles 举例说明猛禽种类。
#### 例6
<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">Amid</span> rumors <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> there were prehistoric mammoths <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">wandering</span> around the unknown region and <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> somewhere in its wilds was a mountain of rock salt 80 by 45 miles in extent, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the two captains set out</span>.
- 主干：The two captains set out. → 两位船长出发了。
- 拆分：Amid rumors ... 是句首状语；两个 that 从句并列，说明 rumors 的内容。
- 内部结构：wandering ... 修饰 mammoths；somewhere in its wilds was a mountain ... 是倒装，主语是 a mountain of rock salt。
#### 例7
In the seventeenth century <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the organ, the clavichord and the harpsichord became the chief instruments</span> of the keyboard group, a supremacy they maintained <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">until</span> the piano supplanted them at the end of the eighteenth century.
- 主干：The organ, the clavichord and the harpsichord became the chief instruments of the keyboard group. → 三种乐器成为主要键盘乐器。
- 拆分：a supremacy ... 补充说明前面的主导地位；they maintained ... 是修饰 supremacy 的定语从句，省略了关系代词。
- 时间：until ... 表示这种地位一直持续到18世纪末被钢琴取代。
#### 例8
<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">If</span> the pores are large, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the water</span> in them <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">will exist</span> as drops <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">too</span> heavy for surface tension to hold, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">and it will drain away</span>; <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">but</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">if</span> the pores are small enough, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the water</span> in them <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">will exist</span> as thin films, <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">too</span> light to overcome the force of surface tension <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">holding</span> them in place.
- 主干：两组 if 条件句由 but 连接，对比孔隙大、小两种情况：大孔隙中水形成水滴并流走，小孔隙中水形成薄膜。
- 拆分：too heavy for surface tension to hold 表示水滴太重，表面张力无法托住；too light to overcome ... 表示薄膜太轻，无法克服表面张力。
- 修饰：holding them in place 修饰 surface tension，说明表面张力把薄膜固定在原处。
#### 例9
In a countercurrent exchange system, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the blood vessels</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">carrying</span> cooled blood from the flippers <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">run</span> close enough to <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the blood vessels</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">carrying</span> warm blood from the body <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">to pick up</span> some heat from the warmer blood vessels.
- 主干：The blood vessels run close enough to the blood vessels to pick up some heat. → 两组血管距离足够近，可以交换热量。
- 拆分：两处 carrying ... 分别修饰两组 blood vessels，说明它们输送冷血和温血。
- 结构：close enough ... to pick up ... 表示靠得足够近，从而能从较暖的血管吸收热量。
#### 例10
For example, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">some early societies ceased</span> to consider certain rites essential to their well-being <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">and abandoned them</span>, <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">nevertheless</span>, <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">they retained</span> as parts of their oral tradition <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">the myths</span> <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">that</span> had grown up around the rites <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">and admired them</span> for their artistic qualities <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">rather than</span> for their religious usefulness.
- 主干：Some early societies ceased to consider certain rites essential and abandoned them; nevertheless, they retained the myths and admired them. → 一些社会放弃仪式，但保留并欣赏相关神话。
- 拆分：consider certain rites essential 是“认为某些仪式很重要”；essential 作宾语补足语。
- 修饰：that had grown up around the rites 修饰 myths；as parts of their oral tradition 说明以口头传统的形式保留神话。
- 对比：rather than ... 表示欣赏的是艺术价值，而非宗教用途。

## 第六讲 时态
### 一、先看懂动词形式
do：原形；does：第三人称单数；did：过去式；done：过去分词；doing：现在分词。这里用它们代表各种动词的形式。
- <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">一般</span>：事实、习惯，或把动作作为一件事来叙述。
- <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">进行</span>：某个时间正在进行。
- <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">完成</span>：从某个时间点回看之前的动作或状态。
- <span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">完成进行</span>：强调此前一段时间持续进行的活动。
### 二、常用时态

| 时态 | 结构 | 核心意思 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 一般现在 | do / does；am / is / are | 事实、习惯、现在的状态 |
| 一般过去 | did；was / were | 过去发生的事或状态 |
| 一般将来 | will do；am / is / are going to do | 将来的预测、意愿或计划 |
| 现在进行 | am / is / are doing | 现在或现阶段正在进行 |
| 过去进行 | was / were doing | 过去某时正在进行 |
| 现在完成 | have / has done | 过去与现在有联系：结果、经历或持续 |
| 过去完成 | had done | 到过去某时已经发生，即“过去的过去” |
| 现在完成进行 | have / has been doing | 从过去持续到现在或刚结束，强调过程 |

#### ① 一般现在、一般过去、一般将来
- Water <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">expands</span> when it freezes. → 科学事实，用一般现在。
- The species <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">disappeared</span> thousands of years ago. → 过去发生的事，用一般过去。
- The team <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">will collect</span> more samples next year. → 将来的行动，用一般将来。
#### ② 现在进行、过去进行
- The climate <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">is changing</span>. → 现阶段的变化，不一定只指说话这一刻。
- The researchers <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">were collecting</span> samples when the storm began. → 暴风雨开始时，采样正在进行。
#### ③ 现在完成：重点
- Researchers <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">have discovered</span> a new species. → 过去发现，现在已有这项发现；强调结果。
- This species <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">has lived</span> here for millions of years. → 从过去一直生存到现在；强调持续。
**完成不等于“结束”**：has lived 可以表示现在仍然生活在这里。
常见线索：since + 起点，for + 时长，so far（到目前为止）；要结合句意判断。
#### ④ 过去完成：重点
- By the time the team arrived, the lake <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">had frozen</span>. → 湖先结冰，团队后到达。
先找到过去的参照点，再看哪个动作更早；并非所有先发生的动作都必须用过去完成。
#### ⑤ 现在完成进行
- Scientists <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">have been studying</span> this species for years. → 多年来一直在研究，强调持续的过程。
可以仍在继续，也可以刚刚结束；不必理解为“一定还没停”。
### 三、三组易混对照
#### ① 一般过去 vs 现在完成
- I <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">lost</span> my key yesterday. → 说昨天发生的事。
- I <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">have lost</span> my key. → 强调现在钥匙丢了、找不到。
yesterday、last year、in 1997 等已结束的过去时间，通常搭配一般过去。
#### ② 一般过去 vs 过去完成
- When we arrived, the lecture <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">began</span>. → 我们到达时，讲座开始。
- When we arrived, the lecture <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">had begun</span>. → 我们到达前，讲座已经开始。
#### ③ 现在完成 vs 现在完成进行
- I <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">have read</span> three papers today. → 强调成果：读了三篇。
- I <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">have been reading</span> papers all morning. → 强调过程：一上午都在读。
### 四、其他形式：能认出即可

| 形式 | 结构 | 简短理解 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 过去完成进行 | had been doing | 到过去某时，之前一直在做 |
| 将来进行 | will be doing | 将来某时正在做 |
| 将来完成 | will have done | 到将来某时已经完成 |
| 将来完成进行 | will have been doing | 到将来某时，已持续做了多久 |
| 过去将来 | would do / was going to do | 从过去的角度看将来 |

例：He said he <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">would return</span>. → “返回”在“说”之后；would 在这里表示过去将来，不是虚拟语气。
### 五、被动语态：和时态一起认
结构：<span style="color: var(--color-cyan, #40e0d0)">be + done</span>；时态由前面的助动词形式体现，done 不随时态改变。

| 动词形式 | 意思 |
| --- | --- |
| is / are studied | 被研究（一般现在） |
| was / were studied | 被研究（一般过去） |
| is / are being studied | 正在被研究 |
| was / were being studied | 过去某时正在被研究 |
| has / have been studied | 已经被研究／研究持续至今 |
| had been studied | 到过去某时已经被研究 |
| will be studied | 将被研究 |

- The samples <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">have been tested</span>. → 已经接受检测；have been tested 整体是谓语。
- The samples <span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">being tested</span> are from the lake. → being tested 修饰 samples；真正谓语是 are。
### 六、阅读时怎么用
① 先圈完整谓语：<span style="color: var(--color-yellow)">may have been discovered</span> 要一起看，表示“可能已经被发现”。
② 再判断时间、主被动和动作先后；同一段可用一般过去叙述研究过程、一般现在说明结论，不必强行统一时态。
